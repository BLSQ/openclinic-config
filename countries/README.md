# countries/

One directory per country, named by its country package: `countries/<c>/<c>/` is the country
package, and beside it everything made for that country alone, its facility types (`base: <c>`), its
layers (`country: <c>`), its sites' own packages and its projects. A country's reviewer owns its
directory (`.github/CODEOWNERS`). The rules are L1 to L8 of the README.
